Our Mission

SoloDesk provides free, professional tools and resources to help freelancers and solopreneurs run their businesses more efficiently. We believe every independent professional deserves access to quality business tools without barriers.

What We Offer

  • Free online business calculators and generators
  • Professional invoice and quote tools
  • Time tracking for better productivity
  • Educational guides and articles
  • Business templates

Privacy First

All our tools run entirely in your browser. We don't collect, store, or transmit your business data. Your calculations and documents stay on your device.

100% Free

SoloDesk is completely free to use. No sign-ups, no subscriptions, no hidden fees.
